# 04: Flow credit accounting doesn't match the per-clip quote (observation)
- **Observation:** the pilot charged 20 credits per Veo 3.1 Fast clip (1,050 → 1,030 → 1,010). The 4 clips for scenes 02–03 then moved the balance only 1,010 → 980 (30 credits, not the 80 expected).
- **Possible causes (unverified):** daily free credits (+50/day) landing mid-batch, a delay in the balance display, or a discount/refund.
- **Impact:** none on the results. Cost reporting in the post uses the quoted 20 credits/clip and notes the discrepancy.
- **Status:** open observation.

## Update 13:44 IST (batch 3)
Scenes 04–06 were submitted one at a time, with the balance read before and after each submit. Every clip cost exactly the quoted 20 credits (980 → 860 for 6 clips). The 30-for-4 anomaly in batch 2 did not reproduce with sequential submits. It most likely comes from submitting several clips at once (queueing) or from a lag in the credit ledger, not from per-clip pricing.
Totals for the experiment: 12 clips, 1,050 → 860 = **190 credits** (the 2 pilot clips at 40, the batch of 4 at 30, then 6 clips at 120).
Status: an observation only. Results are unaffected, since every clip used identical settings.
